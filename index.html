Dual Tap

Dual Tap is a fast-paced, hyper-casual mobile game. The purpose of this app is to provide players with an entertaining tapping experience where they try to survive, collect 'Yang', and test their reflexes.

The game uses Google Play Games Services to allow players to log in securely and compete with others on the global high-score leaderboard.
